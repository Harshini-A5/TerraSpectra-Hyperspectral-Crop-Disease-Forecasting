\# TerraSpectra Dataset Selection



\## Selected Dataset

Early Detection of Septoria Infection on Wheat Leaves Using Hyperspectral Imaging Data.



\## Source

https://entrepot.recherche.data.gouv.fr/dataset.xhtml?persistentId=doi:10.57745/WVP0FJ



\## Dataset Details

\- Crop: Durum wheat

\- Disease: Septoria tritici blotch

\- Samples: 3,326 leaves

\- Conditions: Healthy and infected

\- Acquisition dates: 18 dates

\- Spectral types: VNIR and SWIR

\- Data type: Hyperspectral images



\## Reason for Selection

This dataset provides genuine hyperspectral measurements of healthy and infected wheat leaves. Its multiple acquisition dates also provide an opportunity to investigate disease progression.
